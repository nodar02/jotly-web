    Questions

    Does Jotly connect to my bank?

    No. You jot what you spend, so nothing needs your bank login. Automatic jots from Apple Wallet payments are coming soon.

    Where is my data stored?

    On your iPhone, synced through your own iCloud account. You can export everything to CSV at any time.

    Which currencies does it support?

    Jot in any currency. Pick a main currency for totals and budgets; each jot keeps its original amount and the rate from that day.

    Is there an Android version?

    Jotly is iPhone-only for now, in English, Russian and Spanish.
