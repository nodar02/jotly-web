Privacy Policy

Jotly is built so your spending stays yours. This policy explains what the app stores, what reaches us, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Your jots live on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.
  • We can’t see your expenses, and we never sell data or show ads.
  • Jotly never asks for your bank login.
  • Apple handles payments — we never see your card.

01What Jotly stores on your device

When you jot, the app saves the amount, currency, exchange rate, note, category, account, date and time, plus your budgets and settings. This data is stored on your iPhone.

If iCloud sync is on, the same data is kept in your private iCloud database so it can appear on your other Apple devices. Apple stores it under your Apple ID; we (the developer) cannot read it.

02What we receive

  • Subscription status — Apple tells the app whether your trial or subscription is active. We do not receive your name, card or billing address from Apple.
  • Exchange rates — once a day the app downloads public currency rates from an exchange-rate service. The request contains no personal information or expense data.
  • Messages you send us — if you email support or use the contact form, we receive your name, email and message, and use them only to reply.

03Analytics and advertising

Jotly shows no ads and contains no advertising trackers.

04Face ID and device security

If you turn on Face ID lock, the check is done by iOS. Jotly only learns whether it succeeded; it never receives biometric data.

05Your choices

  • Export all your jots to CSV from Profile at any time.
  • Delete all app data from Profile → Security & data, or by deleting the app.
  • Remove the iCloud copy in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Jotly.
  • Turn iCloud sync off in Settings at any time; your data then stays only on that device.

06Children

Jotly is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

07Changes to this policy

If we change how Jotly handles data — for example if we add an optional Jotly account with server sync — we will update this page, change the date above and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

08Contact

Questions about privacy: support@jotly.info. Data controller: the developer of Jotly.
